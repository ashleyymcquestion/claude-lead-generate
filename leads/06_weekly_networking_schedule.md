# 06 - Weekly Orange County Networking Schedule

Prepared: 2026-10-13. Audience targets: real estate investors, active traders, CPAs, and other people likely to be accredited investors.
A daily 10am (Pacific) push notification re-checks these and sends the day's list (see "Daily notification" below).

**Confirm before you go.** Details come from web search results (Meetup, OCBJ calendar, organizer listings), not from calling each group. Recurring schedules change; call the number or check the link the day before.
**At every event:** collect contact info only. No pitching returns, no taking money or documents (506(c) rules in `02_events_and_networks.md`). Scrub numbers against DNC before any follow-up call.

## Where the accredited investors are (ranked)

1. **OCREIA (2nd Thu)** and **OC FIBI (1st Tue)**: established real estate investors with capital in deals.
2. **CPAs**: CalCPA Orange County / Long Beach chapter "ABC Night" mixers (attorneys, bankers, CPAs; last one May 28, 2026 at Bosscat, Irvine; fall date not yet posted; contact Madison.Foster@calcpa.org). Chamber leads groups are the weekly way to meet CPAs.
3. **CFA Society OC, FPA OC, ACG OC**: advisors and dealmakers who can refer clients.
4. **OC Traders**: active traders; many are high net worth, but screen.
5. **Tech Link Up, Tech Coast Angels (Irvine)**: founders and angels with liquidity events.

## Daily notification

A Routine runs every day at 9:57 AM Pacific (so the push lands around 10 AM). Each run re-checks today's and the next two days' events from this list, searches for new OC investor/CPA/trading events, and sends a push notification with event, time, location and phone number.
