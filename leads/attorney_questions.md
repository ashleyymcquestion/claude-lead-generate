# Questions for Securities Counsel: Outreach to the PFD Call Sheet

*Prepared for PFD Capital Partners. This is a list of questions for your attorney. It is not legal advice.*

## 1. Background to give your attorney (copy this in)

- PFD Capital Partners is based in Irvine, CA. We are raising capital under **Rule 506(c)**, with a **$100,000 minimum** investment, from **verified accredited investors**.
- The investment funds medical receivables for personal-injury doctors. It is secured with a 3:1 collateral setup, and we describe the return as a **target**, not guaranteed.
- We built a call sheet of **239 professional firms in 40+ states**: CPAs, estate and trust attorneys, independent RIAs and wealth managers, physician advisors, a few family offices and angel groups. We want to call their **published main business lines** and, where we can find them, email their general business addresses.
- The goal is to ask these firms whether they would be open to **introducing** their accredited clients, or to learn about the offering. We are **not** asking them to buy.
- The numbers came from public search listings and directories. None has been verified on the firm's own site yet.
- Attached: `PFD_Call_Sheet.pdf` and `PFD_Call_Sheet_Details.xlsx`.

## 2. Questions to ask

### A. Phone calls
1. Do federal Do-Not-Call rules (TCPA and the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule) and California's rules **apply to calls to a firm's main business line**? Is there a business-to-business exemption, and does it hold when the call concerns a securities offering?
2. Do we still need to scrub the call sheet against the **National and California Do-Not-Call lists** and keep an **internal** do-not-call list? Which states in our list have their own rules?
3. What are the **permitted calling hours**, and who may place the calls?
4. Do we need to **record or disclose** that calls may be recorded? Which states require two-party consent?
5. What should a caller say at the start of the call (company name, purpose), and what should they **not** say about returns, collateral and safety?
6. Where a number turns out to be an individual's direct line or mobile, what is the rule?

### B. Emails
7. Does **CAN-SPAM** apply to an email to a firm's general business address? What must every email include (physical address, opt-out, accurate sender)?
8. Do California or other states' rules limit **emailing a named partner**, as opposed to a general inbox?
9. Which email address sources are safe? Is it fine to use addresses published on a firm's own site, and not fine to use harvested or purchased ones?
10. How long can we keep a person on the list, and how fast must we honor an opt-out?

### C. Securities rules (506(c))
11. Because 506(c) permits general solicitation, does that cover this kind of **outreach to firms**? Do we need any written materials approved before use?
12. If a firm **introduces a client**, what do we need to do before taking money (accredited verification method, subscription documents, bad-actor checks)?
13. Can we offer a referral firm **anything of value**? Could paying a referral fee make the firm an unregistered broker-dealer, or put our exemption at risk? If we want a paid structure, what is the compliant route (registered placement agent or broker-dealer)?
14. Are there restrictions on **what the referring firm may say** to its clients? Are CPAs, attorneys and RIAs limited by their own professional rules on recommending investments or accepting referral compensation?
15. Does contacting firms in **other states** trigger state notice filings or registration? Which states, and when (at first sale in the state)?
16. Which **Form D** and **California notice** filings are required, and by when?

### D. Content and claims
17. Please review our **scripts, emails, landing page and FAQ** (`04_outreach_playbook.md`, `landing_page.html`).
18. What do we need on hand to **substantiate** the claims we make about the 3:1 collateral and the target return?
19. What **risk disclosures** must accompany any mention of the return?
20. Can we describe the offering to a referral firm in a short pitch before they have seen the PPM, or only after?

### E. Health-care angle
21. Because investor money funds **personal-injury doctors**, are there **anti-kickback, fee-splitting or lien-related** issues if physician advisors, practice brokers or medical groups refer investors?
22. Should we keep the **investor message** separate from what we say to **providers**?

### F. Records and process
23. What **records** should we keep for each contact (date, who, what was said, consent, DNC check)?
24. Should we **limit the call sheet** (for example, drop wirehouse-affiliated firms, or certain states) until you sign off?
25. Do we need an **internal compliance policy** for calls and emails, and who should review it?

## 3. Two things to ask for in writing
- A **go / no-go** for each outreach channel (phone to firms, email to firms, direct to individuals).
- A short **written checklist** of steps to complete before the first call.

## 4. Before the meeting
- Scrub the numbers against the National and California DNC lists (this needs your own registered account).
- Spot-check about 20 numbers on the firms' own websites.
- Bring the current scripts and landing page.
- Write down the **states** you most want to reach.
